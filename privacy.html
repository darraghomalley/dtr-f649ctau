Double Triple Rainbow - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

In short

Double Triple Rainbow does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your game in progress, your high scores, your past scores and your settings (such as language and sound) are saved only on your own device, by the game itself. They are never sent anywhere. Uninstalling the game removes them.

Sharing a score

If you choose to share a score, the game makes a short video of it on your device and hands it to the app you pick (for example WhatsApp). Nothing is sent unless you tap Share and choose an app. What happens after that is covered by that app's own privacy policy.

Internet

The game does not need the internet to play and does not send any data about you or your games.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If the game ever adds something that does use data (for example optional ads or purchases), this page will be updated first, and the game's Google Play listing will say so.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer through the contact details on the game's Google Play listing.
